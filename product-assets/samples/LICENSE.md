# Cannon in D

`cannon-in-d.mxl` was supplied for use as the Zupulse demo score. Its embedded MusicXML identifies its source as
<http://musescore.com/user/17746751/scores/3581731> and does not declare a license.
The bundled copy adds a `work-title` matching the existing “Cannon in D” title credit so the application can
display the title consistently; its musical content is unchanged.

The composition is public domain; the licensing status of this specific arrangement is unspecified.
